About

Background

I'm a designer working at the intersection of visual design, UX, and emerging technology. I hold a BFA from the University of Michigan and am currently completing my Master of Science in UX Research and Design at the same institution.

This past summer, I interned with Lucidity, an AI-powered marketing analytics tool, where I explored what it means to design for AI rather than just with it by building a reusable style library to support AI-generated reports. What began as a straightforward styling project grew into something bigger. It gave me a chance to rethink how design systems hold up when the "user" generating the output is a model, not a person. Before that, I worked as a Graphic Designer at Pica Marketing Group, developing visual content using Adobe Creative Suite and Figma.

As I continue with my studies, I aim to deepen my expertise in user-centered design and drive innovative solutions. My background in design, marketing, and user experience reflects a commitment to creating meaningful and engaging designs.
